2026 Taiwan–France Joint Research Workshop
on Cybersecurity & AI

Provisional Program Updated: 6 October 2026

Workshop venue: Institute of Information Science, Academia Sinica, Taipei (5 – 6 November)

OverviewTalk FormatWorkshop Program

1. Overview

Date Program
Nov. 3 (TUE) Visit
Nov. 4 (WED) Visit
Nov. 5 (THU) Malware Workshop + Poster Session; Banquet in the evening
Nov. 6 (FRI) Morning: joint AI Security session; then parallel Security and AI Workshops + Poster Session; Farewell Party in the evening

2. Talk Format

  • Each talk: 15 minutes (12-minute presentation + 3-minute Q&A); talks in the joint AI Security session on 6 November are 20 minutes.
  • Poster Session: held during lunch (12:00 – 13:30) — student posters on 5 November and TACC research posters on 6 November. Presenters are asked to stand by their posters from 13:00 to 13:30.
  • Friday 6 November: after the morning joint session, the Security Workshop and the AI Workshop run in parallel.
  • Note: This is a provisional program. Please feel free to make any changes as needed.
