Taiwan–France Cybersecurity & AI Workshop 2026

Call for Posters for Cybersecurity Track

We invite poster presentations in the Student Poster category. All participants will present their work in poster format at the conference.

On this page: Important Dates Topics Student Poster Submission Format Poster Presentation

Important Dates

Student Poster — Submission Deadline
October 12, 2026
Student Poster — Notification of Acceptance
October 23, 2026

Topics

The Cybersecurity Track welcomes research on a broad range of cybersecurity topics, with a particular interest in malware analysis and detection.

Topics of Interest

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Malware Analysis and Detection
  • Threat Detection and Cyber Threat Intelligence
  • Network and System Security
  • Software and Application Security
  • AI and Machine Learning for Cybersecurity
  • Security Automation and Autonomous Cyber Defense
  • Cybersecurity Data Analytics
  • Emerging Cybersecurity Threats and Defense

Student Poster

This category is open to undergraduate students, graduate students, and postdoctoral researchers. Both individual and group submissions are welcome. Each submission must have a faculty advisor or mentor.

The one-page submission should briefly describe the research background, methodology, and key findings. Previously published or publicly presented work is also welcome. If the work has been published or presented elsewhere, this must be clearly indicated in the submission, and the original publication or presentation venue must also be clearly cited on the poster.

Student Poster submissions are subject to review. Interested students should submit their information and upload the one-page submission through the Google Form.

Submission Length: Maximum 1 page

Review Process

Student Poster submissions will undergo a single-blind review.

Submissions will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

  • Novelty and Significance: The originality or significance of the research idea, approach, findings, or perspective, and its potential contribution to the field.
  • Research Quality: The soundness and appropriateness of the research methodology and analysis.
  • Clarity: The clarity and organization of the research objectives, methods, and findings.
  • Relevance: The relevance of the work to the scope and themes of the conference.

Submission Format

Student Poster submissions should follow the IEEE Conference Template.

Google Form links for submission and registration.

Poster Presentation

All accepted Student Posters will be presented in poster format at the conference.

Poster presentation guidelines, including poster size and format, will be announced at a later date.
